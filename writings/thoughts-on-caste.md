### Thoughts on Caste

Caste has created nations within nations within nations. It is nepotism on steroids. It holds down competence and promotes mediocrity. It straitjackets creativity and innovation. It erects barriers between neighbors. It fuels artificial rage towards fellow men. It is the little brother of religion in terms of menace caused to civilization outstripping perceived benefits. Yet another diabolical human invention to subjugate and dominate other humans, violently if required.
